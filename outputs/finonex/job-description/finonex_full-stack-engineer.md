## Job Description

Location: Herzliya, Tel Aviv District, Israel

Finonex is a long-standing and stable trading-platform company, delivering advanced online trading technology to global partners for over a decade. Looking for a Full Stack Engineer to join a growing team and help build and improve products.

Joining a collaborative global team of 6 engineers working on a strategic solution approaching its first major milestone, continuing to evolve with new features and capabilities. Looking for someone who enjoys ownership, thrives in a dynamic environment, and is excited about building scalable solutions from the ground up.

In this role, you will:
- Own features end-to-end, from database design to business logic to API to UI implementation, responsible for complete feature delivery.
- Drive technical decisions by bringing ideas to feature kickoffs, proposing solutions, and influencing architectural choices.
- Build with modern technologies including React, Node.js, Python, and TypeScript.
- Hit the ground running on a high-impact project and contribute from day one.
- Collaborate closely with a global team while maintaining a high level of independence and accountability.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional full-stack development experience.
- Strong proficiency in Node.js, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, and React.
- Proven ability to work independently and take ownership of complex features.
- Strong problem-solving skills and a self-driven approach to learning and researching solutions.
- Excellent English communication skills.

Nice to have:
- Experience with Kafka.
- Background working on CRM systems or similar enterprise applications.
- Cloud platform experience (AWS, GCP, or Azure).
- Understanding of CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
