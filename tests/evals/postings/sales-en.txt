Account Manager - B2B Distribution (Tel Aviv)

We are a growing B2B distributor of consumer electronics and mobile accessories,
serving retail chains, independent stores, and corporate procurement teams across
Israel. We are looking for an Account Manager to own and grow a portfolio of
existing business customers while bringing in new accounts.

- Track pipeline, forecasts, and KPIs in Excel and our ERP.

Requirements:
- At least 3 years of B2B sales or account management experience - must.
- Proven experience negotiating price and commercial terms with business customers - must.
- Fluent Hebrew and English - must.
- At least 3 years of direct SaaS sales experience - must.
- Salesforce Certified Administrator certification - must.
- Experience with Priority ERP - an advantage.
- Valid driving license and willingness to travel nationwide - an advantage.
