## Job Description

We are looking for a talented and proactive Full Stack Developer to join our Professional Services team. In this role, you won't be coding in a vacuum; you'll be at the forefront of solving real-world business challenges. You will build custom solutions, Design complex integrations, and architect data pipelines that directly impact our clients' success.

What You'll Do

Write clean, maintainable, and efficient code (Node.js, Python) to automate workflows, build custom APIs, and develop client-specific features.
Design queries and manage data across various database systems (SQL-based like PostgreSQL/MySQL and NoSQL-based like MongoDB).
Build scripts to extract, transform, and load (ETL) data between client legacy systems and our core platforms.
Perform root-cause analysis on technical issues within client environments and deploy rapid fixes.

What You'll Bring:

1-3 years of experience in Full Stack development or a similar technical role
Proficiency in Node.js/Python, advantage for candidates with knowledge of .NET.
Basic Web Stack Essentials: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
Hands-on experience with SQL (writing joins, queries) and exposure to NoSQL environments.
Comfortable navigating Linux and Windows environments, with a basic understanding of Active Directory.
Familiarity with Git, Postman, and DBeaver.

Your Technical DNA

Ability to analyze complex, disorganized datasets and design effective strategies to clean and migrate them.
Ability to quickly parse technical documentation to extract specific API endpoints or SQL syntax and implement them on the fly.
Capable of deciphering the logic of existing, undocumented Python scripts and codebases without guidance.
If you thrive on the variety of technical coding mixed with creative problem-solving and client interaction, let's talk.
