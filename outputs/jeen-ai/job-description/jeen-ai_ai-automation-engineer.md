## Job Description

Jeen.AI empowers enterprises with generative AI through advanced AI agents, automations, voice analytics, and knowledge-based insights across any cloud or on-premise environment. Trusted by industry leaders shaping tomorrow's technological landscape.

Why Join us?:

Design prompts, build automations, and ensure AI model quality in a hands-on role. You'll have creative freedom to shape innovative AI products while growing your technical skills in a fast-moving environment.

Responsibilities:

Prompt Creation and Automation Development: Design and refine prompts to improve the performance of AI models and develop automations by integrating various LLM models
Python & SQL Coding: Write and debug code to support the development and enhancement of our AI-driven products
QA Testing: Conduct quality assurance tests on AI models and related software to ensure reliability and accuracy
Research: Stay up to date with the latest advancements in AI and related technologies to inform product development and strategy
Collaboration: Work closely with cross-functional teams, including developers, designers, and product managers, to drive project success

Requirements:

Education: A degree in Industrial or Information Systems Engineering
Languages: Advanced proficiency in both English and Hebrew
Technical Skills: Coding abilities, preferably in Python and SQL
Attention to Details: Ability to meticulously manage and execute tasks with precision
Prior knowledge or experience with AI technologies - must

Preferred Qualifications

Experience in a command/instructional role
Understanding of user interface design principles
