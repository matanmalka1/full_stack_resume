# Job Description — Verix / Junior Professional Services Data Engineer

## Company
Verix

## Role
Junior Professional Services Data Engineer

## About the Company
Verix delivers AI-driven solutions that optimize pharma commercial operations. Their Tovana platform integrates diverse data sources to provide predictive, real-time insights for leading life science organizations.

## Key Responsibilities
- Translate business requirements into AI and BI analytics solutions
- Build and maintain automated ETL pipelines, reports, and queries
- Set up and maintain AI models to support customer needs
- Solve complex technical challenges raised by clients
- Perform data analysis and exploration on large-scale datasets

## Requirements

### Must Have
- Strong SQL skills and hands-on experience with databases
- Bachelor's degree in CS, Industrial Engineering, or related field (or equivalent military experience)
- High level of written and spoken English
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Attention to detail; ability to work independently

### Advantage
- ETL and reporting tools experience
- Python development
- Redshift or PostgreSQL experience
- Experience with large-scale, complex datasets in a business environment

## Key Attributes
- Strong problem solver; independent; creative and practical
- Fast learner; ownership mindset; curious
- Team player
- Ability to deliver under time constraints
