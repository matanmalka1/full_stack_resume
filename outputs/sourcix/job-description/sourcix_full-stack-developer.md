## Job Description

**Location:** Tel Aviv  
**Employment:** Full-Time, On-Site

### The Role

SOURCIX is looking for a Full Stack Developer to build high-quality products and turn complex systems into reliable, intuitive solutions. The developer will work on the core SaaS platform and marketplace, collaborating with product and AI teams. The role includes understanding product requirements, designing and implementing scalable solutions, and delivering features to production.

The developer is also expected to use modern AI tools and rapid, iterative development practices to move quickly in a startup environment.

### Responsibilities

#### Development & Architecture

- Build, maintain, and scale modern web applications across frontend and backend.
- Design and implement end-to-end features, from UI to backend services.
- Translate product requirements into high-quality, reliable solutions.
- Own feature design, implementation, testing, and deployment.
- Ensure system performance, scalability, reliability, and maintainability.

#### Product & User Experience

- Work closely with product and design to build efficient, user-focused workflows.
- Bridge frontend experience and backend logic.
- Simplify complex systems into clear and usable solutions.
- Improve the product continuously based on feedback and data.

#### Modern Development

- Use AI tools, including LLMs, copilots, and automation tools, to improve productivity and code quality.
- Iterate quickly, prototype when needed, and ship production-ready features.
- Stay current with AI-driven development and modern engineering practices.

#### Collaboration

- Collaborate with frontend, backend, and AI engineers.
- Work with APIs, databases, and data flows across the full stack.
- Participate in code reviews and technical discussions.

### Requirements

- 3+ years of software development experience.
- Strong frontend and backend development experience.
- Experience with modern web technologies such as React, Next.js, Node.js, or similar.
- Good understanding of APIs, databases, and system design.
- Experience building and integrating production systems end-to-end.
- Strong attention to detail and code quality.
- Ability to work independently and take ownership.

### Nice to Have

- Cloud infrastructure experience with AWS, GCP, or similar.
- Scalable system design and architecture experience.
- Experience building SaaS platforms or complex systems.
- Familiarity with frontend and backend performance optimization.

### What SOURCIX Is Looking For

- A builder mindset with speed and ownership.
- Strong problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work in ambiguity and shape solutions.
- Curiosity and drive to stay current with AI and modern development.

### Why Join SOURCIX

SOURCIX is an AI startup transforming mechanical procurement and sourcing through a platform for custom manufacturing powered by AI and advanced matching algorithms. The company offers competitive compensation, early-stage equity, product and technology ownership, and work at the intersection of AI, manufacturing, and SaaS.
