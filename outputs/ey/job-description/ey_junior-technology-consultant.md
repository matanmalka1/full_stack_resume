## Job Description

Junior Technology Consultant

About the job
EY's Technology Consulting practice helps leading organizations navigate digital transformation, cloud adoption, and emerging technologies.

We are looking for a Junior Technology Consultant to join our team. This role offers a unique opportunity to work closely with senior architects and technology leaders, gaining exposure to enterprise-scale technology and cloud initiatives.

Responsibilities

Support technology consulting and architecture projects across various industries.
Participate in client meetings to gather business and technical requirements.
Assist in solution design, architecture documentation, and technology assessments.
Research emerging technologies and cloud-based solutions.
Collaborate with client IT, development, infrastructure, and security teams.
Contribute to projects involving cloud platforms, APIs, integrations, and enterprise technologies.

Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field.
1-3 Years of Experience in software development or a technology-focused role.
Familiarity with modern development environments and tools.
Knowledge of cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure) – an advantage.
Understanding of APIs, integrations, CI/CD, SaaS, or PaaS solutions – an advantage.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication skills in Hebrew and English.
Self-motivated, curious, and eager to learn new technologies.
Strong teamwork and client-facing abilities.

Why EY?

Work closely with leading technology architects and consultants.
Gain exposure to strategic projects and top organizations.
Develop expertise in cloud, architecture, and technology consulting.
Enjoy a collaborative environment with strong professional growth opportunities.
