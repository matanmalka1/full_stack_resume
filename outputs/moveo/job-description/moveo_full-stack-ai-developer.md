## Job Description

Join one of Israel's leading banks and become part of its Data + AI Division, driving the next generation of intelligent banking solutions. As a Full Stack AI Developer, you'll help shape the future of AI-powered banking by developing autonomous AI agents, designing scalable enterprise solutions, and bringing innovative AI capabilities from concept to production in a cutting-edge technology environment.

### What You'll Do

- Develop end-to-end Full Stack applications using Angular and Node.js.
- Design, build and deploy autonomous AI Agent solutions.
- Participate in system architecture and technical design from concept to production.
- Build scalable Microservices-based applications.
- Integrate AI frameworks such as LangChain and LangGraph.
- Develop and consume REST APIs.
- Work with SQL Server, MongoDB and Redis.
- Deploy applications to OpenShift (OCP) and AWS environments.
- Collaborate in Agile Scrum teams.
- Work with LLMs, Prompt Engineering methodologies and AI protocols such as MCP.
- Lead cross-functional technical initiatives and drive adoption of best practices.
- Leverage AI development tools throughout the software development lifecycle.
- Ensure high standards of quality, performance, scalability and stability.

### Requirements

- Hands-on experience developing with AI-powered tools such as Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or similar: Must.
- Experience working with AI frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, or similar: Must.
- 3+ years of strong backend development experience, with Node.js preferred: Must.
- 3+ years of strong frontend development experience, with Angular preferred: Must.
- Experience developing REST APIs: Must.
- Strong proficiency in TypeScript, HTML, and CSS: Must.
- Experience working with cloud infrastructure: Must.
- Experience working with CI/CD pipelines and Agile methodologies: Must.
- Strong understanding of software architecture and designing scalable distributed systems: Must.
- Experience leading cross-functional initiatives, driving stakeholder alignment, and presenting technical solutions.
- Hands-on experience integrating third-party products and services.
