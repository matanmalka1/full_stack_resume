## Job Description

Our platform powers a large-scale software-distribution and monetization system: installers and bundlers that deliver freeware to millions of users while presenting curated third-party software offers during setup. You'll join a small, fast-moving engineering team that builds and operates this platform end-to-end.

What you'll do:

- The job involves software development as well as elements of professional services.
- Develop and maintain our proprietary, distribution platform for cyber-security products that will be used by millions of users.
- Maintain and extend our installers across multiple engines.
- Manage, Build and maintain campaign landing pages.
- Write internal automation tools in Python to enhance the team abilities and improve overall workflows.
- Work in a ticket-driven workflow (Jira), collaborating closely with senior engineers on PRs and code review.
- Support the business team by assisting with data driven analytics, investigations and solving everyday challenges.
- Utilize, maintain & handle Agentic systems.

What we're looking for (required):

- Solid fundamentals in at least one general-purpose language (Python, C#, C++, or JavaScript).
- Solid fundamentals in frontend frameworks & language (JS, CSS, React/Angular).
- Familiarity with structured configuration files (YAML/JSON) and reasoning about how configuration drives behavior.
- Familiarity with Git and pull-request workflows.
- Familiarity with GCP / AWS services and best-practices.
- Meticulous attention to detail — With a user-base as big as ours, every small changes matters.
- Clear verbal & written communication.
- High level of analytical and problem-solving skills to effectively analyze and resolve technical issues.
- Fluent in English, written and spoken.

Advantages:

- Up to 2 years of experience in the following domains: Technical Support / NOC / Technical Operations / Integrator.
- Experience with Frontend design and development.
- Windows development / native (C++/C#) background.
- Understanding of registry, OS internals, WinAPI's.
- Familiarity with Ad-tech terminology

What you'll grow into:

You'll gain deep, marketable experience across the full delivery stack: native windows engineering, config-driven platform design, CI/CD automation, cloud infrastructure, micro-services and web development, with a clear path toward mid-level ownership of your own areas of the platform.
